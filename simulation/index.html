1. The Neuron Model (Izhikevich)

We use the Izhikevich Model to simulate realistic spiking behavior. It balances the biological plausibility of Hodgkin-Huxley neurons with the computational efficiency of integrate-and-fire models.

v' = 0.04v² + 5v + 140 - u + I u' = a(bv - u)

v: Membrane potential

u: Recovery variable (membrane capillary restoration)

a, b, c, d: Biologically inspired parameters

Reset condition:
If v ≥ 30 mV, then v ← c and u ← u + d.

Biological (Regular Spiking): a=0.02, b=0.2, c=-65, d=8
Synthetic (Fast Spiking): a=0.1, b=0.2, c=-65, d=2

2. Anatomical Connectome Mapping

The Parietal Connectome module uses real human brain connectivity data (Destrieux Atlas, 150 regions). Unlike the abstract modules, this module implements a direct data-driven mapping of biological architecture into the SNN substrate.

3. Modular Substitution Sequence

Substitution occurs across five modules: Sensory, Connectome, Cognitive, Primary Kernel, and Motor. The process follows a strict sequential order to ensure signal stability and response matching during the transition.

4. State Transitions

  • Biological: 100% organic substrate.
  • Hybrid: Gradual replacement of neurons with synthetic counterparts.
  • Synthetic: Full substrate transition completed.

5. Continuity Integrity
